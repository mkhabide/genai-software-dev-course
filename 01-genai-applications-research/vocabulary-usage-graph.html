Counting method and limitations

  • Explicit frequency: case-insensitive matches of the vocabulary term, including common inflections (for example, prompt/prompts and integrate/integration). A sentence counts once per term even if the term appears more than once in it. Product descriptions in the report are included; headings, URLs, and the reference list are excluded.
  • Implicit frequency: a manual, conservative count of distinct sentences that describe the term's meaning without using its name or a listed inflection. The short note under each term records the basis for these subjective annotations. Implicit counts are interpretations, not automated keyword matches.
  • One sentence can contribute to multiple vocabulary terms. The overview is counted separately from the six numbered application sections. Counts describe this report only and are not measures of general importance or technical quality.
